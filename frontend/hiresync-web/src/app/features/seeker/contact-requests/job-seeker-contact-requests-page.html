About contact requests

Employers can create one contact request for an application. You decide whether its workflow status becomes Accepted or Declined.

  • Review the employer and vacancy
  • Respond only to Pending requests
  • Completed requests cannot be changed again

Privacy by design

Acceptance changes workflow status only. HireSync does not reveal employer email, phone number, messages, attachments or personal contact details here.

Workflow states

A request begins as Pending and can move once to Accepted or Declined. Accepted and Declined are terminal states.
